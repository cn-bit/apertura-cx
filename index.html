Every brand is experienced long before it is chosen, and long after it is paid for.

Customer experience is the full arc of that relationship. First impression and reputation, the enquiry and the visit, the purchase, the handover and the care that follows.

It moves between screen and space without pause. A polished website cannot rescue an indifferent welcome, and an exceptional venue cannot make up for a booking that was never confirmed. Customers experience it all as one. We call it phygital.

The Customer Journey

Six stages every customer passes through, whatever the business. We begin by understanding your journey across them, in the language of your industry. We then audit each stage, review what we find, shape the strategy and see it through to implementation.

We look from the customer's side of the counter.

Most businesses judge their experience from the inside looking out, through reports, dashboards and team meetings. Customers only ever see it from the outside looking in.

We walk the whole journey as they do, online and in person, and return with a clear account of what is working, where it falters, and what deserves attention first.

Every finding is placed against one of three openings.

It keeps the work anchored to commercial reality. You always know what a problem is costing, and what resolving it is worth.

  • Trust

    Where belief is won or lost

    First impressions, consistency, and whether promises are kept. The smallest details, packaging that falls short of the price point or a reply that never arrives, can quietly undo a strong brand.

  • Conversion

    Where interest becomes a decision

    The enquiry, the booking, the inspection, the counter and the checkout. The moments where a customer hesitates, is asked to repeat themselves, or is simply left waiting.

  • Loyalty

    Where a purchase becomes a relationship

    Delivery, aftercare and everything that follows. The most valuable growth in a business is often the customer it has already won.
