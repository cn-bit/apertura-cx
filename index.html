Every brand is experienced long before it is chosen, and long after it is paid for.

Customer experience is shaped across the entire relationship. From the first impression and the initial enquiry to the visit, the decision, the delivery and the care that follows. Each encounter contributes to how a business is understood, remembered and ultimately valued.

It moves between screen and space without pause. A polished digital experience cannot compensate for an indifferent welcome, just as exceptional service cannot make up for a booking that was never confirmed. To the customer, these are not separate experiences. They are one continuous relationship, moving between the physical and digital.

The Customer Journey

Every customer relationship is shaped by a series of encounters, some deliberate, others almost unnoticed. A first impression, a question answered, a welcome, a decision made with confidence, or a reason to return.

We consider these encounters across six stages of the customer journey. Each reveals something about how a business is experienced, and how even the smallest details can influence what happens next.

  1. DigitalIn person

  2. 01Awareness

    The first impression begins before a conversation takes place.

    DigitalA search result, a social post, a review left by a stranger.

    In personA shopfront, a recommendation over dinner, a building that catches the eye.

  3. 02Consideration

    Interest grows through the questions asked and the answers received.

    DigitalAn enquiry form, an email, a message sent late at night.

    In personA telephone conversation, a question at the counter, a first appointment.

  4. 03Experience

    The moment expectations meet reality.

    DigitalA booking journey, a virtual appointment, an inspection arranged online.

    In personThe welcome at the store, the venue or the display suite.

  5. 04Decision

    The point at which confidence becomes commitment.

    DigitalThe checkout, a deposit, a contract ready to sign.

    In personA question resolved, a reassurance offered, the conversation that settles it.

  6. 05Delivery

    The promise made, and how faithfully it is kept.

    DigitalConfirmations, progress updates, a message when plans change.

    In personThe delivery, the meal, the handover of keys.

  7. 06Advocacy

    What people remember, share and choose to return to.

    DigitalA review, a recommendation shared, an invitation to return.

    In personBeing recognised when they return, bringing someone with them.

What a business delivers and what a customer experiences are not always the same.

From within a business, the customer journey is understood through reports, processes and the people responsible for delivering it. Customers encounter something more immediate. The welcome, the unanswered question, the unexpected delay, the small gesture that makes them feel remembered.

Apertura CX examines the journey from the outside looking in, revealing where expectations are met, where the experience falls short and what deserves attention first.

How we measure what matters.

Every interaction influences how a customer perceives a business, whether they choose to proceed and whether they return.

  • Where confidence is earned or lost.

    Trust

    Trust is built through consistency between what a business promises and what it delivers. A thoughtful first impression, a timely response, packaging that reflects the price paid, or a welcome that feels genuinely considered. Small details that collectively shape how a brand is perceived.

  • Where interest becomes a decision.

    Conversion

    Every customer reaches a point where curiosity must become commitment. An enquiry, a booking, an inspection or a purchase. The clarity of the information, the ease of the process and the confidence inspired by each interaction can determine whether they proceed or quietly walk away.

  • Where a transaction becomes a lasting relationship.

    Loyalty

    The relationship does not end when a purchase is made. Delivery, aftercare, recognition and the way a business responds when something goes wrong all influence whether customers return, recommend it to others or gradually drift away.

  • Three measures

    Apertura CX examines the customer journey through three measures of performance. Trust, Conversion and Loyalty. Together, they reveal where the experience strengthens the business, where it creates friction and where meaningful improvement can be made.
