Privacy notice

This notice explains how Apertura CX handles the personal information you share through this website.

What we collect

When you request a Snapshot or send an enquiry, we collect the details you choose to provide. This may include your name, business name, email address, website, industry, location and your message.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your request, confirm scope and next steps, and keep a record of our correspondence with you. We do not sell your information or add you to marketing lists without your agreement.

How it is handled

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through a third-party form service. We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure, and we keep it only for as long as it is needed for these purposes.

Access and questions

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by contacting us.
