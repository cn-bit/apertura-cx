Privacy notice

This notice explains how Apertura CX handles the personal information you share through this website.

What we collect

When you request a Snapshot or send an enquiry, we collect the details you choose to provide in the form. This may include your name, business name, email address, website, industry, physical location, your message and your agreement to be contacted.

Why we collect it

We collect these details so that we can understand your enquiry and respond to it. For a Snapshot request, they help us assess whether the Snapshot suits your business and confirm its scope, timing and next steps.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, confirm the scope and next steps of any work, and keep a record of our correspondence with you. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing communications unless you have separately agreed to receive them.

Who processes it

Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form service, which forwards them to us by email. The Web3Forms privacy policy applies to that processing. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. The website's typefaces are served from the website itself. The website does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising tools.

How it is kept

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure, and we keep it only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above.

Privacy enquiries

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or raise any question about this notice, by contacting us.
